# Hold the Deck — Design Doc

A Grow Castle-style idle defense web game. Players defend a pirate ship on the open sea from waves of drowned sailors and sea monsters, using a crew of heroes found in treasure chests.

(Originally "Kampung Defense", set in a Malaysian kampung; reskinned to pirates with identical mechanics and renamed. The browser save key still uses the old name, `kampung-defense/save`, so existing saves keep loading.)

Passion project — no real money, no ads. Everything is earned through play and should feel generous. The only real-time timers are Expeditions, an optional extra that never gates anything (see Expeditions).

## Tech

- **Engine:** Phaser 3 + Vite (JavaScript)
- **Hosting:** GitHub Pages. Every asset URL (sprites, sheets, fonts and the JSON data files) carries the build's version (`?v=...`, new on each build), so after a deploy browsers don't mix cached old data files with the new code (Pages caches files for 10 minutes). An effect sheet that still fails to load is simply not drawn (with a console warning) rather than showing Phaser's missing-texture box.
- **Saving:** browser localStorage (auto-save), versioned so old saves can be migrated
- **Resolution:** the base resolution is **480x270** pixels, and the game area is always exactly **480** of them wide, scaled (fractionally) to fill the window's width. Between 4:3 and 16:9 (most laptops and monitors) its height follows the window: 270 at 16:9, 300 at 16:10, 360 at 4:3. A window wider than 16:9 shows the 16:9 area (480x270) centred, scaled to the window's height, with bars left and right; one taller than 4:3 shows the 4:3 area (480x360) centred with bars above and below. Nothing is ever trimmed or stretched. The bars are the page's dark navy (`#141a30`) with a thin wooden frame round the game area (`DISPLAY.bars`). Scale and area are re-picked whenever the window, fullscreen state or browser zoom changes. Everything (sprites, UI and text) is drawn at 1x in those pixels (`pixelArt`) and every object is drawn on whole pixels (`roundPixels`).
- **Fullscreen:** a button next to the sound toggle switches the page to fullscreen and back (its icon shows which); Esc also leaves. It is hidden where the browser doesn't support fullscreen (e.g. iPhone Safari).
- **Art:** pixel-art sprites in `public/sprites/`, drawn with Phaser's `pixelArt` setting (nearest-neighbour, whole-pixel positions) at 1x in the base resolution, so pixels stay square. Every hero and enemy has a sprite, as does the ship (one per deck count). Anything added later without art falls back to a coloured rectangle.
- **Battle scene:** `bg_tall.png` (480x430: `bg.png`'s sky, sea and palm island on the right, with more sky above and more sea below; the original picture sits at its y = 100, which lines up with the battlefield's y = 0) behind everything and `fg_sheet.png` (near water and island foliage, 3 frames looping at 3 fps) in front, positioned and layered as `layout.json` describes. An island with its own scenery swaps these for its versions, at the same positions and sizes (Ember Isle: `ember/bg_ember_tall.png` and `ember/fg_ember_sheet.png`; Frostbite Reef: `frost/bg_frost_tall.png` and `frost/fg_frost_sheet.png`, plus a still front layer, `frost/fg_pines.png`; Fogbound Isle: `fog/bg_fog_tall.png` and `fog/fg_fog_sheet.png`), as soon as the ship sails there. The battlefield is a fixed 480x270 world: every gameplay position (ship, crew, spawn point, lane, `shipContactX`, the Kraken's rising spot, the Siren's rock, the Ghost Galleon, flight heights) is its `layout.json` value at any window size or aspect ratio, so walking distances, crew ranges and shot travel never change with the screen. It is always the full width of the game area, so the ship touches its left edge and the island its right. In a game area taller than 270, 60% of the extra height is more sky above and 40% more sea below (`SCENERY` in config: up to 54 above and 36 below at 4:3), and nothing spawns or walks there. Resizing the window mid-wave only re-lays the storm and HUD. Draw order: background, the Frost Wyrm (perched on the background's iceberg, behind the ship) and the Siren Queen (rising in front of the sunken mast, behind the ship), ship, crew, enemies (the Siren among them, her rock under the near water, boarding boats, and the Frost Giant, whose boots the near water hides), The Kraken, the Ghost Galleon and the Molten Leviathan (and the Kraken's splash and the Leviathan's steam), foreground, an island's front layer (Frostbite Reef's pines, over every enemy and boss), ship railing layer, flying enemies and lava bombs, Fogbound Isle's mist and fog banks; slot markers (and lava bomb warnings, ice blocks on frozen crew, charm marks on charmed crew), labels, HP bars, the weather (Ember Isle's ash, Frostbite Reef's snow and blizzards) and the finale's storm, projectiles and UI go on top.
- **Positions (layout.json):** the ship's top-left is at `shipPos`. Walking enemies enter at `enemySpawnX` off the island's beach and follow the lane: their feet y is interpolated between the lane points as they walk, from the sand down into the water. They stop when their front edge reaches `shipContactX`.
- **Ship sprites:** `ship_stage1/2/3.png` (160x160) show the ship with 1, 2 or 3 decks built; the one matching the current deck count is shown. Heroes are drawn between it and the matching `ship_stageN_front.png` railing layer, so they stand behind the railings. Hero slot positions come from `ship_slots.json` (each deck's 2 slots as centre x and feet y in sprite pixels). Where two slots on a deck are close together, the second hero's name label sits higher so the labels don't overlap. Empty slots show a frame and a "+".
- **Character sprites:** heroes and every enemy but nine are 32x32 with the feet on the bottom row, all facing left; they stand on their slot (heroes) or the lane (enemies). The Kraken is 64x64, the Siren 48x48 (on her rock), the Ghost Galleon and the Molten Leviathan 96x80, a boarding boat 32x20 (its bottom on `layout.boardingBoat.waterlineY`, not the lane), the Frost Wyrm 112x96, the Frost Giant 144x144 (standing on its `feet` point, 88,136, not the bottom row), the Siren Queen 96x96 (her waterline, row 86, on the water) and Captain Blackbones 64x64 (on his `feet`, 32,62). The Fog Wraith floats 8px over the lane. The Sled Penguin's sled runners are on row 27 of its frame, so that row stands on the lane (`origin` in config). Each enemy's hit box is its visible body (`width` / `height` in config), measured from its art. The newer enemies (Iron Crab, Storm Harpy, Keg Runner, Barnacle Knight, Siren, boarding boat, and Ember Isle's, whose single PNGs aren't used) are drawn from sheets only. Ember Isle's art lives in `public/sprites/ember/`, Frostbite Reef's in `public/sprites/frost/` and Fogbound Isle's in `public/sprites/fog/` (their enemies' single PNGs are their Wanted Board portraits). Rectangle placeholders stand the same way.
- **Effect sprites:** every projectile, impact, status mark and ability effect is a sheet in `public/sprites/fx/`, drawn at 1x; `fx.json` gives each one's frame size, frame count, fps, whether it loops or plays once (one-shot effects hold their last frame) and what it's for. Shots are drawn flying right: the harpoon, whale harpoon and Duelist's blade arc turn to their flight angle, and the spinning ones (frying pan, grog bottle, stew pot, grog barrel) just loop their animation. Lobbed shots (cannonball, grog bottle, frying pan, stew pot, grog barrel) fly in an arc to where the target will be; the rest fly straight. Where a shot hits, its impact plays: an explosion for cannonballs, a grog splash for bottles (and the Grog Barrel), a hit spark for everything else. Every hit (except poison ticks) flashes the enemy white for 60ms. Enemy effects: a Keg Runner's blast is the explosion at 2x size, a Barnacle Knight's shield breaking plays `shield_break` where the shield was, the Siren's `siren_note` floats from her to the crewmate it stuns, and the Ghost Galleon's `ghost_cannonball`s arc from its gun ports to the hull and explode there. Ember Isle's effects are in `ember/fx/` (`fx_ember.json`): `lava_bomb` and `bomb_warning` for its hazard, `lava_splash` where a bomb or magma glob lands (and where a bomb is shot down), the Leviathan's `magma_glob` and `steam_burst`, and the Magma Crab's `fire_puddle`. Frostbite Reef's are in `frost/fx/` (`fx_frost.json`): the Yeti Raider's `snowball`, the Frost Wyrm's `frost_breath` (its right edge at the Wyrm's mouth), the Frost Giant's `ground_quake` (centre-bottom on its club's slam point) and finale `ice_boulder` (spun in code as it flies), `frost_hit` where a snowball, boulder or the breath lands and when an ice shield breaks or a crewmate is tapped free, `ice_shield` looping over a shielded enemy, `ice_block` over a frozen crewmate, and `snow_puff` off the pines. Fogbound Isle's are in `fog/fx/` (`fx_fog.json`): the Cursed Cannoneer's `grapeshot` (turned to its flight) with `ghost_hit` where it lands (also where a charm is tapped away), the Lantern Ghoul's `soul_wisp`, `fog_puff` as a Fog Wraith fades and as Blackbones sinks and rises, the Siren Queen's `charm_note`s and `siren_wave` (its bottom on the water), `charm_status` over a charmed crewmate, Blackbones' `ghost_slash` on the hull and `summon_circle`s on the lane (his flintlock flashes the crew's `muzzle_flash`, flipped). `ghost_flame` is loaded but not used yet. Crew effects: the parrot, scalpel (turned to its flight) and spinning ghost cutlass fly like the others; the Sharpshooter's `musket_shot` leaves the musket's tip (`muzzle` in config) with a `muzzle_flash` there; the Storm Caller's chain is instant: `lightning_seg` laid end to end along each link (turned to it, flickering, shown 0.2s) and `lightning_hit` on each enemy.
- **Animation:** in battle, characters are animated from `<name>_sheet.png`, with frame sizes, frame lists and fps from `animations.json` (crew), `animations_new_enemies.json` (enemies, plus the Ghost Galleon's `gunPorts`) `ember/animations_ember.json` (Ember Isle's enemies, plus the Molten Leviathan's `mouth` and `position`) and `frost/animations_frost.json` (Frostbite Reef's, plus the Yeti Raider's `handOnRelease`, the Frost Wyrm's `mouth` and `perchPos` and the Frost Giant's `feet`, `slamPoint` and smash `impactFrame`) and `fog/animations_fog.json` (Fogbound Isle's, plus the Cursed Cannoneer's `muzzle`, the Lantern Ghoul's `lantern`, the Siren Queen's `pos`, `mouth` and `waveHand` and Blackbones' `feet`, `muzzle`, slash `impactFrame` and shoot `fireFrame`). The Cinder Skeleton, Magma Crab and Obsidian Golem loop "walk" while moving; the Ash Bat loops "fly" the whole time; the Molten Leviathan loops "idle", plays "spit" for each glob and roar, and "sink" when beaten. The Frozen Corsair and Yeti Raider loop "walk", the Sled Penguin "slide" and the Ice Witch "glide" while moving; the Yeti plays "throw" and the Witch "cast" (then they stand on their first walk frame). The Frost Wyrm plays "fly" while swooping in or lifted off, loops "perch", plays "breath" and, beaten, "fall"; the Frost Giant loops "walk" (a tiny camera shake on walk frames 1 and 3, its footsteps), plays "roar" (also raising its club to throw a boulder), "smash" and, beaten, "shatter". The Bone Deckhand and Cursed Cannoneer loop "walk", the Fog Wraith "glide" and the Lantern Ghoul "walk" while moving; a summoned or raised deckhand plays "rise" first, the wraith "fadeOut" and "fadeIn", the cannoneer "fire" and the ghoul "raise". The Siren Queen plays "rise", loops "idle", plays "sing", "wave" and, beaten, "sink"; Captain Blackbones plays "fogRise" / "fogSink", loops "walk", plays "shoot", "summon" (twice through), "slash" and, beaten, "death", holding its last frame. A charmed crewmate turns to face the ship. Crewmates loop "idle" and play "attack" each time they fire, then return to idle; each one's idle starts on a frame staggered by slot so the crew doesn't bob in sync. The Drowned Sailor, Thief Monkey, Iron Crab and Keg Runner loop "walk" while moving and stand still otherwise; the Barnacle Knight walks with "walk_shield" until its shield breaks, then "walk_noshield". The Storm Harpy loops "fly" the whole time, showing "dive" while it dives. The Siren loops "idle" and plays "sing" while she sings. The Kraken loops "idle" and plays "attack" each time it hits the ship. The Ghost Galleon plays "emerge" once, loops "idle", plays "fire" with each volley and "sink" when beaten; boarding boats loop "row". A crewmate scared by Haunt turns to face the island while fleeing. Animations slow down with the enemy when slowed (and speed up while the Siren hastes it) and freeze while stunned. Characters without an animation fall back to their single PNG (a little lunge or squash when they attack).
- **UI kit:** `public/sprites/ui/` holds 9-slice wood and parchment panels, gold and wood buttons (normal / hover / pressed / disabled images, used for those states), a bar frame and a 12px icon sheet; `ui.json` gives the slice sizes, icon order and colours. Mock-ups: `ui_mock_battle.png` and `ui_mock_between_waves.png` in the project root, and in `mockups/` the Wanted Board, new-enemy alert, finale (`ui_mock_finale.png`) and world map (`ui_mock_world_map.png`).
- **Fonts:** all text is bitmap text from `public/sprites/ui/fonts/`, never scaled (not even in animations: the expedition reveal hides a card's "?" before the card squeezes over) and positioned on whole pixels (centred text included), so it is as crisp as the art. Text that doesn't fit its box gets a wider box or the small font, never a smaller scale. The main and big fonts leave at least 1px between glyphs (and have a redrawn "1"), so numbers such as 116K or 13.6M never run together. Pre-coloured fonts by background:
  - **main_light** (16px, cream with a dark outline): on wood panels and wood buttons, and over the scene. Coloured text (red warnings, rarity names, gold amounts) tints it; the outline stays dark.
  - **main_dark**: on parchment and gold buttons.
  - **small_light** (8px): small text over the scene and on bars. **small_dark**: small text on parchment.
  - **big_light** (32px): SET SAIL!, wave banners, the chest reveal's result line and the new-crewmate title.
- **Hero cards** (chest reveal, new-crewmate splash, Crew Roster): parchment under a wood plate naming the rarity, the hero's single `<name>.png` at 2x (scaled art is kept to whole-number scales: hero cards, the expedition screens' destination pictures at 2x and their 12px icons at 3x), name, pixel stars (with "+N" after them for Bonus Levels), level and (on the large card) effect, with the hero's **trait icon** (see Expeditions) in the top-left corner under the plate. A crewmate away on an expedition gets a dark band over their feet with the hourglass and the time left. Heroes not collected yet are a wood card with a black silhouette of their sprite and "???".
- **Crew faces:** the Shipwright, hero picker, ability bar, Captain's Log, MVP plaque, chest reveal and expedition screens show each hero's face: a 16x14 crop of their single sprite starting at `faceTop` (`SPRITES.heroes` in config), set so tall hats (The Captain's tricorn, the Ship's Cook's toque, the Voodoo Priestess's headdress, the Duelist's plume, the Storm Caller's hood) are cut off and the eyes sit about halfway down. A face whose sprite isn't loaded falls back to a square in the hero's colour (never Phaser's black-and-green "missing" texture).

## Core Loop

1. Player presses **Start Wave**. Hull HP is refilled to full before every wave.
2. Enemies spawn on the right in formations and come left toward the ship.
3. Heroes on the ship auto-attack.
4. **Wave cleared:** earn gold and Pearls (see Currencies) and advance to the next wave.
5. **Hull HP hits 0:** wave fails, wave number does NOT advance. Gold earned from kills during the attempt is kept. Player can upgrade and retry. No other penalty.
6. Between waves: upgrade screen, hero slot assignment, treasure chests.

Waves scale in difficulty (see Waves). The voyage is split into islands of 50 waves, each ending in a finale (see Islands); a cleared island's waves go on endlessly.

## The Ship

- Starts with **1 deck, 2 hero slots**.
- Build up to **3 decks** in v0.1. Each new deck adds 2 slots (max 6) and **+50 max HP**, so a deck is useful even before there are heroes to fill it.
- Upgradeable **hull HP**, compounding: max HP is 120 x 1.07^level, plus 50 per deck built. Each level costs 20 x 1.15^level gold.
- Heroes are assigned to slots by the player between waves (click a slot, pick an owned hero; picking a hero already placed elsewhere swaps them). Only slotted heroes fight.
- **Art:** `ship_stage1..3.png` (one per deck count) and a `_front` railing layer each, all **176x160** and drawn by their top-left at `layout.json`'s `shipPos` (origin 0, 0). The bowsprit at the bow runs to x 171 with a tip cap and the forestay to its tip, and the bow has a carved beakhead for the figureheads; it reaches past `shipContactX` (140), over the enemies that stop there. Deck slots, the contact point and crew positions are unchanged from the 160x160 art. **Ship parts** draw over the stage image, under the crew (see Ship Parts).

### Elites

From wave 20 any enemy but the boss can spawn as an **Elite**: tinted gold, 3x HP (and 3x shield), 3x gold. The chance per enemy is 8% at wave 20, +0.4% per wave after, up to 30%.

- **Boss waves** (every 10th): 65% of the usual enemies, with the boss entering 30% of the way through (an island's finale wave is built differently; see Islands). - **Sirens** join every 3rd wave from an island's first Siren wave (never a boss wave): from 16 on Skull Cove (16, 19, 22…), from 7 on Ember Isle (7, 13, 16…); none on Frostbite Reef or Fogbound Isle (the Siren Queen is a boss, not a Siren).
- **Scaling** (n = wave - 1): enemy HP x (1 + 0.5n + 0.012n²), about 6.5x at wave 10, 15x at wave 20, 39x at wave 40 and 72x at wave 60 (it used to be 1 + 0.2n); enemy damage x 1.04ⁿ (about 1.4x at wave 10, 2.1x at 20, 6.6x at 49) up to effective wave 100 (island offset included, so all of Skull Cove and Ember Isle), then x1.03 per wave after it but never above **x62** (`WAVES.lateDamage`), or the island's own cap (`damageCap`): Frostbite Reef's damage rises from x49 on its wave 2 to x62 on its wave 10 and stays there, instead of reaching x319 by its finale; Fogbound Isle's cap is **x68**, which the curve (x201 at its wave 1) is past from the start, so its damage is x68 all island; kill gold and the wave-clear bonus x 1.07ⁿ. Keg Runners and boarding boats don't use the damage multiplier: they hit for a share of the hull.
- **Endless grace:** after an island's finale is beaten, its next 10 waves bring fewer enemies (and Elites), 50% of the usual count on the first one, rising evenly toward the full count, so Endless doesn't open with a spike.
- **Flyer warning:** pressing SET SAIL! for a wave with flyers (Storm Harpies, Ash Bats) when nobody on the ship can hit them (no anti-air crew and no Net Thrower) asks first: "Flyers ahead!", with Cancel / Set sail.

### Balance simulation

`npm run sim [-- runs maxWave [voyage]]` plays the game headless from a fresh save (the real battle code, with Phaser stubbed out; see `scripts/sim/`). A bot opens every affordable chest (Buy 10 whenever it can), puts its best crew on the ship (The Captain beside the strongest, someone anti-air from wave 8) and either spends all its gold after every wave (**greedy**) or only after losing one (**lazy**: its waves between losses show how often upgrades are needed). `SIM_CONFIG` takes JSON merged into config, for trying numbers. The bot plays Skull Cove from wave 1; winning wave 50 clears it (reward included) and it carries on in Endless mode, or with `voyage` it sails on to Ember Isle and plays that up to maxWave. A run stops at a wall: one wave lost 15 times in a row (the bot never sails back to farm). The numbers were last tuned with it in the rebalance described in `BALANCE_REPORT.md`; since Ember Isle the sim also reads the Ghost Galleon's 4 real gun ports (it used to fire 1 ball per volley), which makes Skull Cove's wave 49–50 a little harder there. `npm run sim -- 5 50 voyage` (5 seeds per bot):
- **Skull Cove:** greedy clears it in 3 of 5 runs (the others stall at The Kraken on 30 and on wave 49, 18 Storm Harpies and a Siren); lazy in all 5. Losses come almost entirely from Storm Harpies and leaking Keg Runners, worst on Kraken waves (10, 30) and Siren waves. The roster is complete around wave 39–50. Boss kills take about 20–35s, the finale's Galleon up to about 50s.
- **Ember Isle** (8 runs got there) is harder: 1 clears it (finale included), the rest stall on Ember waves 22, 32, 42 or the Eruption. Its first 20 waves go smoothly; walls come from Ash Bats and Elite Keg Runners in big waves, and in the Eruption from the enraged Leviathan's globs (a two-minute fight). Lava bombs alone rarely sink the ship once someone anti-air is aboard.
- Chest luck still moves a run a lot.
- **Frozen and charmed crew in the sim:** like a player, the bot taps a frozen or charmed crewmate free 0.8s after it freezes or is charmed, 60% of the time (`TAP` in `simulate.mjs`); otherwise the ice or charm lasts its full time.
- **Frostbite Reef:** `npm run sim:frost [-- seeds replays]` (`scripts/sim/frost.mjs`; set `FROST_CACHE` to a file to reuse the end-of-Ember crews between runs) takes the crew the greedy bot has on reaching Frostbite Reef (allowed 100 tries a wave on the way) and plays it two ways: **no upgrades** (that crew plays each listed wave on its own) and **upgraded** (it sails on through waves 1–50 in order, spending after every attempt like the greedy bot: chests with Pearls, then with gold a deck and always the cheapest of hull and crew levels; a wave lost 5 times running is skipped). Each upgraded attempt has its own seed, so a change to one wave doesn't reshuffle the luck of the waves after it. It reports wins, clear times, ship HP left, boss time-to-kill and freezes per wave for both, side by side, plus the Frost Giant's fights (whether he reached the ship, his smashes, and how long he spent scared, pushed, stunned or slowed), with waves 20 and 40 also played without the Ghost Pirate (swapped for the best benched crewmate at his level and stars, same luck). `BALANCE_REPORT.md` section 7 is the first look, section 8 the rebalance and section 9 the early-wave and Giant tuning. With the current numbers, upgraded wins waves 1–9 losing 2–35% of the hull, wave 10 12 times in 15, the Giant's waves 14 and 10 times, and the finale 14 times.
- **Fogbound Isle:** `npm run sim:fog [-- seeds replays]` (`scripts/sim/fog.mjs`; `FROST_CACHE` reuses `sim:frost`'s end-of-Ember crews, `FOG_CACHE` keeps its own end-of-Frostbite crews between runs) plays the same two profiles from a crew like Frostbite Reef wave 50 would have: the end-of-Ember crew, taken through Frostbite Reef exactly like `sim:frost`'s first upgraded replay (marked cleared even if it lost the finale). It reports wins, clear times, ship HP left, boss time-to-kill, charms and hull damage by source per wave (charmed crew apart from the Siren Queen's waves, Blackbones' slashes apart from his flintlock), boss fights and the finale. `BALANCE_REPORT.md` section 10 has the tuning. With the current numbers: no upgrades wins waves 1–9 every time losing 10–38% of the hull, the wave-10 Siren Queen 12 times in 15, the wave-20 Blackbones 9 times, then falls off from wave 28 (none from 33); upgraded wins 1–20 every time, 12–15 times on each of 28–49, and the finale 11 times.
- **Voyage:** a greedy run that clears Frostbite Reef sails on to Fogbound Isle (tagged "Fo" in the output, Frostbite Reef being "F").
- **Packs:** the bot can only open chests from unlocked packs, and opens each from the one with the most crew it doesn't have yet or can still star up (the first on a tie). `npm run sim:packs [-- seeds]` (`scripts/sim/packs.mjs`) plays each pack crewmate against an existing one of the same rarity and role on its pack's island, in the same crew slot beside The Captain, over a spread of waves (boss waves included): wins, damage share and boss time-to-kill (see BALANCE_REPORT.md sections 14 and 15). `npm run sim:stars [-- trials]` (`scripts/sim/stars.mjs`) counts the chests (no battles, every pack open, picked like the bot) until the main six are all at 3 and 5 stars.
- **Ship parts:** `parts=kraken_figurehead:3,ghostweave_sails:5` (after runs and maxWave, e.g. `npm run sim -- 2 30 parts=kraken_figurehead:3,ghostweave_sails:5`) starts every run with those parts made to those levels and on the ship. Without it no part is ever made, and every result is exactly as before parts existed (`npm run sim -- 3 50 voyage` matches wave for wave, hull to six decimals). The bot collects boss materials but never crafts. `npm run sim:parts [-- seeds trials]` (`scripts/sim/parts.mjs`; `PARTS_CACHE` keeps its crews between runs) measures each part on its own island's boss waves and how far Boss Hunts go; `BALANCE_REPORT.md` section 11.
- **The Abyss:** `npm run sim:abyss [-- crews dives]` (`scripts/sim/abyss.mjs`; `ABYSS_CACHE` keeps its crews between runs) takes the greedy bot's crew at the end of Fogbound Isle (5 seeds, a wave lost 30 times skipped, no parts) and dives 3 times per crew (15 seeds) in three profiles: **none** (no spending), **upgraded** (spends between depths) and **parted** (upgraded, with the Kraken Figurehead, Frostwing Sails and Heartwood Cannons at level 5; `parts=` changes them). Options: `profile=`, `from=20` (a checkpoint start) and `seed=N` (one dive per crew with a line per depth: types, currents, boss, and what hit the hull). It reports the depth reached and the total dive time, where dives end (boss, currents), what sinks the ship and which currents end dives per 100 depths met. With the current numbers: none **17.9** on average (38 min at x1), upgraded **29.7** (20–38, 75 min), parted **39.9** (33–50, 108 min); the best four part sets are within 1.6 depths of each other (Kraken + Frostwing + Magma 41.2), none past 53; `BALANCE_REPORT.md` sections 12 and 13.

## Heroes

All heroes are ranged and attack from the ship, except The Captain, who supports. If a shot's target dies while it is in flight, the shot switches to the nearest living enemy it can hit (a lobbed cannonball bends its arc to land on it); shots only fizzle when no enemies are left.

**Anti-air:** the Cabin Boy, Parrot Keeper, Harpooner, Voodoo Priestess, The Duelist and the Storm Caller can hit flyers (Storm Harpies, Ash Bats) in the air, and falling lava bombs; the rest only hit enemies on the lane (their area hits skip flyers overhead). The Net Thrower can't hurt a harpy in the air either, but aims at them (preferring ones still flying), because his net grounds them. Anti-air crew and the Net Thrower carry a small blue mark (an up-arrow in a ring) in the top-right corner of their card and after their name in the hero picker, and their effect line says "hits flyers" / "nets ground flyers".

| Anchor Bosun | **Drop Anchor** | `anchor_drop` (64x64) slams down bottom-centre on the frontmost enemy on the lane; at its impact frame (0.3s) it hits for 8x his damage, stuns 2s and shoves it 20px back, with a shake (bosses resist the stun and shove as usual) | 20s |
| Powder Monkey | **Powder Barrage** | 6 powder bombs lobbed from him, 0.12s apart, to spots spread along the lane; each blasts everything within 20px for 2x his damage | 18s |
| Fire Breather | **Inferno** | Three `inferno_wall` tiles side by side on the lane, centred at x=300, for 5s: ground enemies in it burn for 1.5x his damage per second, and keep burning 3s after they're through | 22s |
| Blacksmith | **Temper Steel** | Whole crew +25% damage for 7s, `temper_aura` on each crewmate (stacks with The Captain's deck buff and other damage) | 26s |
| Whaler | **Whale Hunt** | One `whale_spear` at the enemy with the most HP (bosses and flyers included) for 10x his damage, through armour, with a light shake | 24s |
| Aurora Seer | **Aurora Veil** | `aurora_veil` tiled across the sky while it lasts: every enemy on screen but the bosses is **frozen** for 2.5s (`ice_block` over it); bosses are slowed 40% (chill, halved like any slow) | 30s |
| Banshee | **Wail** | `wail_ring` from the ship (left edge 40px short of where walkers stop, drawn at 3x as its sheet asks): every enemy within 200px of the ship takes 3x her damage, and walkers are shoved 30px back and scared for 2s (bosses resist). Needs an enemy that near | 22s |
| Bone Caller | **Raise the Crew** | 3 friendly skeletons rise from `summon_circle`s at x=170 (14px apart) and walk out to fight for 12s (see Friendly skeletons). Needs a walker on the lane | 30s |

- **Scaling:** damage from abilities is a multiple of the crewmate's own damage, so it grows with level and stars like their attacks do. Durations (and the Hot Stew stun) are multiplied by 1 + 2% per level above 1 + 10% per star, up to 2x. Speed boosts stack multiplicatively (Rapid Fire during All Hands! is 4.5x).
- **Using them:** every ability is ready at the start of each wave. An ability only fires with an enemy to use it on: any targetable enemy on screen, or for the attack speed boosts (Rapid Fire, All Hands!) one within 350px of the crewmate. Pressing one that can't fire plays a dull blip and keeps it ready. Stun and slow from abilities are halved on The Kraken like any other.
- **Flyers:** only Rapid Fire, Big Net, Hex, Lunge, All Hands! and Flock reach (and count) flyers in the air (Flock flyers only, not lava bombs); Big Net grounds them like the Net Thrower's shots. Hot Stew, Grog Barrel, Whale Harpoon, Broadside, Deadeye, Haunt and Tidal Wave work on the lane only.
- **Siren stuns:** a crewmate stunned by the Siren's note can still use their ability.
- **Auto:** a toggle at the end of the ability bar fires every ability as soon as it is ready and has a target. Saved with progress (save v7).
- **Feedback:** the ability name floats up from the crewmate in its colour, with a synthesized sound per ability (blips, a whoosh and clang, a net swish, a barrel crash and glugs, a ringing harpoon, an eerie hex, cannon booms on each impact, a sword "shing", a bugle call). Effects: Rapid Fire's streaks behind the Cabin Boy and All Hands!' sparkles over each crewmate while they last; the stew pot and grog barrel spin through the air and splash (stew splash, grog splash); Big Net drops from above the view onto the lane (tiled along it), lies there while the slow lasts and fades out as it ends; the poison puddle lies on the lane while it lasts and fades out; the whale harpoon skims the lane, turned to its slope, with a hit spark on each enemy; Hex plays its cast on the Priestess (centred on her, its ring at her feet), then a hex burst on each enemy as the curse lands, and the curse mark once the burst is over; a gold buff ring loops under the feet of every boosted crewmate (Rapid Fire, All Hands!, The Captain's deck buff), and The Captain always stands on a still, 60%-dimmed one as the buff's source; a target marker loops on the enemy the Duelist's next Lunge shot will hit (the toughest) while crits remain; Broadside's cannonballs drop onto the lane and explode; each Lunge crit flashes a cross on the target.

- **Durations:** Temper Steel, Aurora Veil, Wail's scare, Inferno and the skeletons' 12s grow with ability scaling like every other duration, and Drop Anchor's stun like Hot Stew's.

### Friendly skeletons

The Bone Caller's skeletons (`ALLIES.skeleton`, `src/entities/Ally.js`) are the game's one friendly unit on the lane:

- Each rises (`ally_skeleton_sheet` "rise", 0.67s, out of reach meanwhile) from a summon circle, then loops "walk", facing right, along the lane toward the island until the first ground enemy ahead, and hits it every 0.9s for 0.6x the Bone Caller's damage (credited to his ability in the Captain's Log). It has 4x his damage in HP, so both grow with his level and stars (and The Captain's buff). A small green HP bar shows over it.
- **Walking enemies that reach a skeleton stop and fight it** instead of walking on (bosses that walk too: the Frost Giant, Captain Blackbones); a Keg Runner blows up on it (no gold, no hull damage). Flyers, boarding boats, The Kraken and anything anchored pass it by, and it doesn't go for them.
- It crumbles (fading out) when its 12s are up or it's beaten; the end of a wave clears them all.
- Drawn in front of the enemies and behind the foreground.

Rarities: Common, Rare, Epic, **Legendary**. Each hero has a placeholder pirate catchphrase in config.

Heroes have:
- **Level:** raised with gold. +20% base damage per level.
- **Stars:** raised by duplicate pulls from packs, max 5. Each star adds a bigger damage bonus than the last: total bonus +10% / +25% / +45% / +70% / +100% at 1–5 stars.
- **Bonus Levels:** a duplicate of a 5-star hero gives +1 Bonus Level: +5% damage each, on top of level and stars, with no cap. Shown as "+N" after the stars (cards, hero picker).

## Currencies

- **Gold:** earned per kill and per wave cleared (10 + 4 per wave after the first, x 1.07ⁿ like kill gold). Spent on hull HP, building decks, hero levels. Thief Monkeys steal a share of it.
- **Pearls:** spent on treasure chests. Earned from:
  - **Start:** 3; with the first waves' Pearls the first chest (8) can be opened after wave 5.
  - **Every wave clear:** +1.
  - **Milestone waves** (every 5th): +3 on top.
  - **Boss kills:** +5, once per boss wave (killing the boss and then losing the wave doesn't pay it again on the retry).
  - **Bounties** (Wanted Board): 10 / 25 / 50 for defeating 25 / 250 / 1,000 of one enemy type.
  - **Island finales:** 40 on top of the wave's own, plus a free Legendary chest (Skull Cove and Ember Isle alike); Frostbite Reef's pays 60 and 2 free Legendary chests.
  - **Expeditions:** Pearls runs, mixed runs and bonus finds (see Expeditions).
- Expeditions also bring gold (scaled to the player's own gold per minute) and free chests.
- **Boss materials:** one per boss, from boss kills, Boss Hunts and now and then expeditions; spent with gold on ship parts (see Ship Parts).

## Treasure Chests

(Called "packs" in the code: `src/systems/Packs.js`, `PACKS` in config.)

- Each chest gives 1 hero, from a **pack**: before opening, the player picks which pack it comes from (Battle Cats style). Each pack only rolls its own crew. The packs, their banners (120x48) and crew lists are in `public/sprites/packs/packs.json`:

  | Pack | Unlocks | Crew |
  |---|---|---|
  | **Sea Dog Chest** | always | Cabin Boy, Ship's Cook, Net Thrower, Grog Brewer, Harpooner, Cannoneer, The Captain, The Duelist, Voodoo Priestess, Parrot Keeper, Ghost Pirate, Storm Caller, Anchor Bosun, Powder Monkey (14) |
  | **Fire & Ice Chest** | reaching Ember Isle (Skull Cove cleared) | Sharpshooter, Ship's Doctor, Fire Breather, Blacksmith, Whaler, Aurora Seer (6) |
  | **Cursed Chest** | reaching Fogbound Isle (Frostbite Reef cleared) | Banshee, Bone Caller, Ghost Pirate, Voodoo Priestess (4; the last two are in the Sea Dog Chest too, so it isn't only two crewmates and their stars can be chased here) |

  "Reaching" an island is its being open on the map. So the Sharpshooter and Ship's Doctor now come after Skull Cove. (The Storm Caller was in the Fire & Ice Chest at first; without him, every Skull Cove run the sim didn't clear stalled at wave 49, 18 Storm Harpies and a Siren, so he moved to the Sea Dog Chest: see BALANCE_REPORT.md 14.3.)
- **Odds:** each pack uses the normal rarity weights, Common 45%, Rare 30%, Epic 18%, Legendary 7%, over the rarities it has crew of (a roll of a rarity it has none of is rolled again, which comes to renormalizing): the Sea Dog Chest's are those; the Fire & Ice and Cursed Chests (no Commons) are Rare 54.5%, Epic 32.7%, Legendary 12.7%, the Fire & Ice Chest's one Legendary being the Aurora Seer (with the Abyss blessing below: Rare 48.4%, Epic 29%, Legendary 22.6%; the Sea Dog Chest Common 42.1%, Rare 28%, Epic 16.8%, Legendary 13.1%). Within a rarity each of the pack's crew is equally likely.
- **Abyss blessing:** once the Abyss's best depth reaches **25**, Legendary weight is doubled in every pack, for good (the best depth never goes down). Each pack's odds line shows "ABYSS BLESSING: LEGENDARY X2" once it's earned, and a greyed "REACH ABYSS DEPTH 25: LEGENDARY X2" before.
- **Free chests:** an island's finale reward (Legendary chests), expeditions' finds and Abyss milestones, in three tiers: a **Chest** (rolls like a bought one), a **Rare Chest** (Rare or better: the Common weight is dropped and the rest renormalized) and a **Legendary Chest** (always a Legendary; every pack has one). They open from **whichever unlocked pack the player chooses**. While any is waiting the open button says "Open free x2" (no Pearls) and the pity line says how many are waiting ("2 FREE LEGENDARY CHESTS - OPEN FROM ANY PACK", or "3 FREE CHESTS - NEXT: RARE CHEST" when the tiers are mixed); they open before any paid chest, the best tier first, and they count on the Chests button's badge. Every free chest counts toward the pity counter like a bought one (a Legendary resets it, and a free Chest or Rare Chest can be the guaranteed Legendary).
- **Pity:** a Legendary is guaranteed within 30 chests, counted across every pack. The counter resets on any Legendary (natural or guaranteed) and is shown in the chest screen ("Legendary guaranteed within N chests"). It only updates after the reveal so it never spoils a natural Legendary.
- Costs 8 Pearls, or **Buy 10** for 72 (all from the chosen pack). It was 5 and 45 before packs; packs made the voyage much easier, and the price is what brought it back (BALANCE_REPORT.md section 15).
- **New packs:** when a pack unlocks (an island's finale won), a "NEW PACK: FIRE & ICE CHEST" banner shows after the CLEARED banner, the Chests button gets a red dot (top-left, beside its count badge) and the chest screen opens on that pack the next time, its banner dotted, until it's been looked at. A save from before packs gets the banner (and dot) for packs it has already unlocked when it loads. Otherwise the chest screen opens on the last pack opened from.
- **Existing saves keep every crewmate they own**, whatever pack each is in now.
- **New hero:** unlocked, added to the roster, and automatically placed in the first free slot if there is one.
- **Duplicate:** +1 star for that hero. A duplicate of a 5-star hero gives +1 Bonus Level instead ("+1 BONUS LEVEL!").

### The chest screen

**Pack carousel → pack details → open.**

- **Carousel** (top): each pack's 120x48 banner with its name in the game's font on a dark strip along its top, the chosen one in the middle and the others either side, shaded. A locked pack has `banner_locked.png` over its banner and its unlock condition ("REACH EMBER ISLE") on a strip along the bottom. Arrows either side, or a swipe across the banners, turn it.
- **Details** (parchment under it): the pack's odds per rarity, the Abyss blessing line, a cell per crewmate (their 32x32 sprite over a strip in their rarity's colour: owned ones in full colour with their stars, the rest dark silhouettes with a "?"), "CREW FOUND 3/6", the Pearls held and a chest's cost, and the pity / free chest line. The Materials strip stays down the left edge.
- **Open** (bottom): Open (Pearls, or "Open free xN"), Buy 10 and Done, from the chosen pack; a locked pack's Open says "Locked". While a chest opens the carousel and details give way to the chest (the title plate shows the pack's name); after the reveal, a tap (or turning the carousel) brings them back ("TAP TO SEE THE PACK").

### Opening a chest

1. **Build-up:** the treasure chest (the kit's chest icon at 6x) rattles harder (it hops) and glows brighter. Rarer pulls build up longer and shake harder (Common 0.7s / Rare 1.3s / Epic 2.0s / Legendary 2.9s). The glow, and light leaking out of the chest, start white and shift to the rarity colour over the last 45% of the build-up, so the player gets a hint just before the reveal. A synthesized rattle-and-rising-hum plays over it; the hum rises higher for rarer pulls.
2. **Open:** with a whoosh, rays of light in the rarity colour spill out, and the hero's card rises out of the chest.
3. **Reveal:** a chime that gets longer and higher with rarity (Legendary adds a sustained chord and a warm full-screen flash); the card glows in its rarity colour. New heroes also get a sparkle sound and a **confetti burst** (40 / 90 / 160 / 280 pieces for Common / Rare / Epic / Legendary).
4. **New-hero splash:** the first time a hero is pulled, a big intro card appears over rotating light rays: "NEW CREWMATE!" ("NEW LEGENDARY CREWMATE!" for Legendaries), the card with name and rarity, and the hero's one-line **pirate catchphrase** (stored per hero in config). Click to continue. Duplicates skip the splash.

- **Buy 10** (the button between Open and Done): opens 10 paid chests at once for 72 Pearls (a free Legendary chest still opens on its own). There's no build-up: the 10 pulls appear as a grid of small parchment tiles where the chest stood (rarity stripe, face, short name, rarity, and NEW! / N STARS / BONUS +N), with the best rarity's chime (and the Legendary flash), a "N NEW CREWMATES!" line, and then the new-crewmate splash for each new hero in turn.
- Pacing (from before packs, with 14 crewmates in one chest): a full roster took about 80 chests (median; 47–140 for 80% of players), with the Pearl income set so it came together around waves 40–55. With 22 crewmates in three packs, the Fire & Ice and Cursed crew can only be pulled from their islands on. Most of the income is wave clears and bounties: 10 waves pay about 21 Pearls before bounties (10 per-wave + 6 milestone + 5 boss).

## Islands

The voyage is split into **islands** of 50 waves each (`ISLANDS` in config, in `map/map.json`'s order). There are four: Island 1, **Skull Cove**, Island 2, **Ember Isle**, Island 3, **Frostbite Reef**, and Island 4, **Fogbound Isle**, the last. The ship (decks, hull), crew, upgrades, gold and Pearls carry over between islands; each island keeps its own wave counter, best wave (the highest it has cleared) and boss-Pearl record.

- **Later islands start harder:** enemy HP, damage, kill gold, wave-clear gold and the Elite chance on an island are those of wave + its `waveOffset` (Skull Cove 0; Ember Isle 49, so its wave 1 is as hard as Skull Cove's wave 50; Frostbite Reef 98, so its wave 1 is as hard as Ember Isle's wave 50; Fogbound Isle 147, so its wave 1 is as hard as Frostbite Reef's wave 50), while each island's own formations, Sirens and bosses arrive on its own waves. The wave line-up is seeded by the wave and the offset.
- **What an island fights:** its own formations, bosses and first Siren wave, with Skull Cove's as the default; optionally its own scenery (with a front layer), weather (ash, snow, mist), damage cap and a hazard (see Ember Isle, Frostbite Reef and Fogbound Isle).
- **Unlocking:** Island 1 is always open; each later island opens on the map when the one before it is cleared. Only islands with content (`available`) can be sailed to: Frostbite Reef opens once Ember Isle is cleared and Fogbound Isle once Frostbite Reef is. (The map's **COMING SOON** card, for an island without `available` content, is still there for any island added later.)
- **Endless mode:** once an island's finale is beaten, its waves go on past 50 as an endless voyage (bosses every 10th wave as usual). Cleared islands can be sailed back to from the map at any time; their counter carries on where it was.

### The finale: Wrath of Skull Cove (wave 50)

Matches `mockups/ui_mock_finale.png`.

- **Storm:** setting sail on wave 50 brings in the storm from `storm/storm.json`, fading in over 1.2s: a dark blue tint over the battle, `storm_clouds.png` along the top of the sky (the top of the game area), `rain_sheet.png` tiled over the whole game area (4 frames at 12 fps), and a lightning bolt at a random x in the sky every 4–8s (the first sooner) with a 0.1s white flash and a thunder crack. It sits over the battle but under projectiles and the HUD, runs on real time, and fades out when the wave ends.
- **Banner:** "WRATH OF SKULL COVE" with "WAVE 50 - FINAL BATTLE" under it, held for 3s.
- **The bosses:** the Ghost Galleon emerges 1.5s in; 20s later The Kraken surfaces in front of it. Each has 150% of its usual HP for the wave. A full wave's worth of regular formations trickles in alongside (100% of the wave's usual count), a group every 3.2s from 5s in.
- **Enrage:** when one boss dies the other **enrages** ("THE KRAKEN ENRAGES!" / "THE GHOST GALLEON ENRAGES!"): tinted red and attacking 1.6x as fast (The Kraken's hits; the Galleon's volleys and boats), its animations sped up to match. If the Galleon falls before The Kraken has surfaced, The Kraken arrives enraged.
- **Beating it:** the storm clears and a "SKULL COVE CLEARED!" banner (held 4.5s) shows the reward: the wave's usual Pearls plus **40 Pearls** and **a free Legendary chest** (waiting in the chest screen), and "WORLD MAP UNLOCKED". Losing it just clears the storm; the next try brings it back.
- The boss Pearls (+5) are paid once, for whichever boss dies first.

### Ember Isle

Island 2, unlocked by clearing Skull Cove. Map card: hazard "Lava bombs", boss "Molten Leviathan".

- **Scene:** `ember/bg_ember_tall.png` (a dusk sky, a smoking volcano and a dark lava sea) and `ember/fg_ember_sheet.png` replace Skull Cove's, at the same layout positions and with the same fixed-width scaling. **Ash** (`ember/ash_sheet.png`, four 64x64 frames at 6 fps) is tiled over the whole game area, over the battle but under projectiles and the HUD, the whole time the ship is there (between waves too); it fades in and out over 1.2s when sailing there or away, and runs on real time (`ASHFALL` in config).
- **Enemies:** Cinder Skeletons, Magma Crabs, Ash Bats and Obsidian Golems, with Keg Runners, Barnacle Knights and Sirens (see Enemies and Waves).
- **Lava bombs** (the hazard): during every wave, every 8–12s (at random), `bomb_warning` appears on a random built deck and a `lava_bomb` falls there from above the sky, landing 1.2s later. Landing, it takes **6% of the hull's max HP** (not scaled by the wave), **stuns that deck's crew for 1s** (stun stars, no attacks) with a `lava_splash`, a camera shake and a hiss. While falling it's an airborne target: **anti-air crew can shoot it down** (any hit pops it, in a `lava_splash` in the air, harmlessly), and they aim at it first since it's nearest the ship. When several fall at once (the Leviathan's roar) they go to different decks while there are enough, then up to 8px apart. Bombs aren't enemies of the wave: they don't count in "N ENEMIES LEFT", don't hold up a win (any still falling vanish when the wave ends), pay no gold and count as no kill, but hull damage from them shows as "Lava Bomb" in the Captain's Log.

### The finale: Eruption (Ember Isle wave 50)

- **The volcano erupts:** a red tint fades in over the battle and the ash gets heavier (two more layers of it, offset from the first and drifting down), and **lava bombs fall every 3.5–4.5s**. It calms when the wave ends.
- **Banner:** "ERUPTION" with "WAVE 50 - FINAL BATTLE" under it, held for 3s.
- **The boss:** 3.5s in (after the banner) the Molten Leviathan rises **enraged** ("MOLTEN LEVIATHAN ENRAGES!"): tinted red, with 150% of its usual HP, spitting 1.6x as fast (its roars keep their pace). A **strong escort** trickles in alongside: 130% of the wave's usual count, a group every 2.8s from 4s in.
- **Beating it:** the eruption calms and an "EMBER ISLE CLEARED!" banner shows the same kind of reward as Skull Cove's: the wave's usual Pearls plus **40 Pearls** and **a free Legendary chest**, and "FROSTBITE REEF UNLOCKED" (on the map, coming soon).

### Frostbite Reef

Island 3, unlocked by clearing Ember Isle (wave 50). Map card: hazard "Blizzards", boss "Wyrm & Giant".

- **Scene:** `frost/bg_frost_tall.png` (an aurora sky, a big iceberg in the bay and a snowy shore with pines on the right) and `frost/fg_frost_sheet.png` (3 frames, animated like Skull Cove's) replace Skull Cove's, at the same layout positions; the lane is Skull Cove's. Over them, and only here, `frost/fg_pines.png` is drawn above every enemy and boss but under the ship's railings and the weather, so enemies coming in on the right **step out from between the trees**: each one that comes in sends a `snow_puff` off the pines at a random point in x 420–470, y 150–220 (`spawnPuff` in config; not the Frost Wyrm, which flies in, or the Frost Giant, which shakes the pines itself). **Snow** (`frost/snow_sheet.png`, four 64x64 frames at 6 fps) is tiled over the whole game area the whole time the ship is there, like Ember Isle's ash (both are `src/weather.js`, with `ASHFALL` and `SNOWFALL` in config).
- **Enemies:** Frozen Corsairs, Sled Penguins, Yeti Raiders and Ice Witches, and the Frost Wyrm and Frost Giant (see Enemies and Waves).
- **The first waves are tougher** (`earlyBoost`): wave 1 brings 2x the usual number of enemies with 2.6x their HP, fading evenly back to the usual on wave 16, so waves 1–15 aren't a walk at Ember-50 strength and there's no jump between 15 and 16.
- **Blizzards** (the hazard): from wave 6, **every 4th wave except boss waves** (6, 14, 18, 22, 26, 34, 38, 42, 46, and on in Endless) a blizzard rolls in **8s** into the wave and lasts **10s**: `frost/blizzard_sheet.png` (4 frames at 10 fps) scrolls diagonally over the snow under a pale tint, fading in and out over 0.4s, with a gust of wind. **Every 3s** of it a random crewmate who isn't frozen yet **freezes for 2s**. Meanwhile **enemies walk 15% slower** (walking only, not attacking).
- **Frozen crewmates** (the blizzard, the Wyrm's breath, the Giant's smash): can't attack (their cooldown waits) and stand in a pale `ice_block` with their animation paused; their ability still works, as when stunned. **Tapping (clicking) a frozen crewmate breaks the ice** at once, with `frost_hit` on them and a crack. Any ice is gone when the wave ends.

### The finale: Wrath of the Reef (Frostbite Reef wave 50)

- **A blizzard the whole wave**, rolling in as the wave starts, but without its freezes: only the bosses freeze crew. Enemies walk 15% slower the whole time (the Giant too).
- **Banner:** "WRATH OF THE REEF" with "WAVE 50 - FINAL BATTLE" under it, held for 3s.
- **Both bosses together:** the Frost Wyrm at 120% of its usual HP for the wave, the Frost Giant at 90% with 60% of its damage (`hpFactor` / `damageFactor` on its finale entry). the Frost Wyrm swoops in 3.5s in (landing at 5s); the Frost Giant comes in behind the pines at 4.6s and steps out of the trees 5s after the Wyrm landed, and can be hit from its roar. Each shows its own banner as it arrives. Neither enrages. Small trickles of regular formations come alongside (60% of the wave's usual count, a group every 3.5s from 6s in).
- **The Giant's boulders** come every 6s here (8s on his own waves).
- **Beating it** (both bosses and the trickle): the blizzard clears and a "FROSTBITE REEF CLEARED!" banner shows the reward: the wave's usual Pearls plus **60 Pearls** and **2 free Legendary chests**, and "FOGBOUND ISLE UNLOCKED": the Reef's flag goes up on the map and Fogbound Isle opens there.

### Fogbound Isle

Island 4, the last, unlocked by clearing Frostbite Reef (wave 50). Map card: hazard "Fog banks", boss "Siren & Blackbones".

- **Scene:** `fog/bg_fog_tall.png` (a moonlit night, a sunken mast in the bay and a skull rock on the shore) and `fog/fg_fog_sheet.png` (3 frames, animated like Skull Cove's) replace Skull Cove's at the same layout positions; the lane is Skull Cove's. **Mist** (`fog/mist_sheet.png`, four 128x48 frames at 5 fps) is tiled in a strip along the waterline, its top 34px above it, scrolling slowly left, over the enemies, crew and ship but under status marks and enemy HP bars, the whole time the ship is there (`MIST` in config, `src/fog.js`). It fades in and out when sailing there or away, like the ash and snow.
- **Enemies:** Bone Deckhands, Fog Wraiths, Cursed Cannoneers and Lantern Ghouls, and the Siren Queen and Captain Blackbones (see Enemies and Waves).
- **Damage cap:** enemy damage is capped at **x68** here (see Waves, Scaling).
- **The first waves are tougher** (`earlyBoost`): wave 1 brings 1.75x the usual number of enemies with 2x their HP, fading evenly back to the usual on wave 12.
- **Fog banks** (the hazard, `src/systems/Mists.js`): on the same waves as Frostbite Reef's blizzards (every 4th from 6, never a boss wave: 6, 14, 18, 22, 26, 34, 38, 42, 46, and on in Endless) a fog bank rolls in **8s** into the wave and lies for **10s**, with a low foghorn: `fog/fogbank_sheet.png` (four 128x128 frames at 6 fps) tiled over the whole view and drifting, fading in over its first second and out over its last, over the battle and crew (at the mist's depth) with enemy HP bars and status marks still on top. While it's in, **the crew's attack range is 30% shorter**, so crewmates on the higher decks may not reach the back of the lane (Cursed Cannoneers at x 300, Lantern Ghouls at x 350). It runs on the battle's time.
- **Charmed crewmates** (the Siren Queen's song): for 3s a charmed crewmate turns to face the ship, `charm_status` loops over their head, and **their attacks hit their own hull** at a random spot (their own shot, lobbed or straight, or the Storm Caller's bolt at once) for their normal damage. They aren't frozen or stunned, and their ability still works. **Tapping (clicking) them breaks the charm** (with `ghost_hit` on them), like the ice blocks; a tap breaks both if they're also frozen. In the Captain's Log their hull damage counts as the Siren Queen's. **For 3s after a charm ends** (worn off or tapped) that crewmate can't be charmed again (`CHARM.immuneMs`), so the same one isn't charmed twice in a row; this goes for the Abyss's Siren's Call too. Charms end with the wave.
- **Graves:** a Bone Deckhand that dies leaves a grave for 8s, which a Lantern Ghoul can raise (see Enemies). Graves go when the wave ends.

### The finale: The Last Voyage (Fogbound Isle wave 50)

- **A fog bank the whole wave**, rolling in as it starts, but only **15% off the crew's range** so it's fair.
- **Banner:** "THE LAST VOYAGE" with "WAVE 50 - FINAL BATTLE" under it, held for 3s.
- **Both bosses together**, each at **120% of its usual HP**: the Siren Queen rises 3.5s in, and Captain Blackbones steps out of the fog 6s later. Each shows its own banner as it arrives. Neither enrages. A trickle of **Bone Deckhands only** (singly or in threes, `FOG_FINALE_FORMATIONS`; 80% of the wave's usual count, a group every 3.5s from 6s in) comes alongside.
- **When the Siren Queen dies, Blackbones raises his crew at once** (as soon as he's free; or as he arrives, if she dies first), on top of his own raisings at 66% and 33%.
- **Beating it** (both bosses and the trickle): the fog lifts and a "FOGBOUND ISLE CLEARED!" banner shows **the biggest reward in the game**: the wave's usual Pearls plus **100 Pearls** and **3 free Legendary chests**, and "THE SEAS ARE YOURS". Fogbound's flag goes up on the map. Auto-continue pauses, and 2.5s later the ending plays.
- **The ending** (`EndingScene`, `ENDING` in config): the world map with every island, its flag and the whole route sailed in red dots; the ship token sails the route from Skull Cove to Fogbound Isle over 9s (each flag bobs up as it passes), then on off the map's right edge. 1.5s in, a wood plate fades in with "HOLD THE DECK" over "THE SEAS ARE YOURS" (the bitmap fonts have no dash), with a gold **CONTINUE** button that closes it. After that every island stays open as before: Fogbound Isle goes on in Endless like the others.

Opened from the **Map** button in the bottom bar (left of SET SAIL!), which appears once an island is cleared, with a red dot until the map is first opened. Matches `mockups/ui_mock_world_map.png`: `map_bg.png` under a "THE CURSED SEAS" wood plate and an X button (top-left, 24px) to close, with the **Expeditions** button (top-right; see Expeditions) opening the board over the map.

- **Ship token** (`ship_token_sheet.png`, 2 frames at 2 fps) beside the current island, bobbing 1px.
- **Island card:** clicking an unlocked island rings it with gold dots and shows its parchment card: name, "ISLAND n - WAVES 1-50" (or "ENDLESS" once cleared), hazard, boss, best wave ("-" if none) and the wave it's at, with **SET SAIL!**, which sails there (with a "SAILING TO …" banner; the backdrop and ash change to the island's) and closes the map. ## The Abyss (endless endgame)

Depth after depth of mixed enemies from all four islands until the ship sinks, a boss every 10 depths, stacking **currents** that remix the islands' hazards, and a best-depth record. Rules in `src/systems/Abyss.js` (pure functions of a dive's seed and depth), the currents in `src/systems/Currents.js`, the end-of-run screen in `AbyssEndScene`; numbers in `ABYSS` (config); art and data in `public/sprites/abyss/` (`abyss.json`: the currents' names, effect lines and icon order in `currents.png`).

- **Unlock:** when Fogbound Isle's finale is won. The ending's **CONTINUE** opens the world map, which plays a one-time reveal: the map zooms 2x on the Abyss's spot as its silhouette fades into its art, under "SOMETHING STIRS IN THE DEEP..." with a CONTINUE button; then it zooms back out and the Abyss's card opens. A save that had already won the finale gets the reveal the next time the map opens (`abyss.revealed`).
- **On the map:** `map_abyss.png` (64x52, a maelstrom) centred at **(420, 200)**, off the route (no line goes to it). Until it opens it's a dark silhouette (tinted `lockedTint` at 80%) with "???" and can't be selected; then "THE ABYSS" under it. The ship token sits by it during a dive.
- **Cards move:** the island and Abyss cards go on the right (as before) when what's selected is on the map's left half, and on the left (`MAP.card.leftX`) when it's right of x 240 (Frostbite Reef, Fogbound Isle, the Abyss), so a card never covers what's selected, and with a left-half island selected nothing on the right half is covered for long: the Abyss's icon is always one tap away.
- **The Abyss card:** "The Abyss", "THE ENDLESS DIVE", **BEST DEPTH**, **DIVES** (started), **START AT** buttons (1 and the checkpoints, the chosen one gold; at most four: 1 and the three deepest), the **CURRENTS** the dive will meet at that depth (its icons; from depth 1, "AT DEPTH 5" and the first current to come; the card rolls the next dive's seed, so this is exactly what it will meet) and a gold **DIVE!**. During a dive it shows "NOW AT DEPTH n" and **BACK TO THE DIVE** instead, and sailing to an island or starting a Boss Hunt asks "Leave the Abyss?" first: leaving ends the dive (it counts toward the record) with a "SAILING TO …" banner and "LEFT AT DEPTH n" (or "NEW RECORD - DEPTH n").
- **The arena:** `bg_abyss_tall.png` / `bg_abyss.png` and the animated front layer `fg_abyss_sheet.png` (3 frames, like `fg_sheet.png`), the same lane geometry as the islands; enemies come out of the glowing cave on the right. Rising glowing motes (`motes_sheet.png`, four 64x64 frames at 5 fps, tiled; `MOTES`, through `src/weather.js`) over the scene the whole time. The basalt pillar's flat top is where Frostbite Reef's iceberg top was, so the Frost Wyrm perches there unchanged; the Siren Queen rises at her Fogbound spot (a sunken mast there too); the Kraken, Ghost Galleon and Molten Leviathan use their usual sea positions and the walkers (Frost Giant, Captain Blackbones) walk the lane. The ship stays at its island during a dive (the Abyss isn't an island in `ISLANDS`); the scene swaps backdrop and weather to the Abyss's (`GameScene.battleIsland`, an island-shaped stand-in from `abyssIsland`).
- **HUD:** the wave plaque reads **"ABYSS · DEPTH 23"** with `icon_depth.png` in the skull's place (red "BOSS" on boss depths; past its width, the maelstrom's map art stands in as for islands). The active currents show as 12px icons on a small wood strip under the hull plaque (bounty toasts move down under it); tapping one shows its name and effect under the strip for 2.5s. Between depths the strip shows the next depth's currents, and its island weather (snow for Blizzard, ash for Volcanic, mist for Fogbound) is already there. SET SAIL! reads **DIVE!**.
- **Depths:** one depth = one wave; a dive goes 1, 2, 3… until the ship sinks. Upgrades, chests, crew and everything else work between depths as between waves. **Depth 1 is Fogbound Isle wave 40**: its enemy count (5 + 2.5 per wave: 103), spawn interval (0.7s) and HP and kill-gold scaling (effective wave 187); every depth after is one wave on (depth d plays as Fogbound wave 39 + d, effective wave 186 + d). **Damage:** depth 1 has Fogbound wave 40's (x68, its cap), then **x1.03 per depth with no cap** (`damageGrowth`, the islands' late growth), so the Abyss gets you eventually: x88 at depth 10, x119 at 20, x160 at 30, x289 at 50, x1,270 at 100. **HP** also grows **x1.03 per depth** on top of the wave curve (`hpGrowth`; x1.75 at depth 20, x2.4 at 30), against the Abyss Tide's crew damage. **Gold:** kill gold and clear bonuses are **x8** in the Abyss (`goldMult`), so upgrading between depths keeps up; a checkpoint's gold and the shallows' gold are x8 too.
- **Enemies:** each depth draws from **16 island enemies**, four per island: Skull Cove's Iron Crab, Storm Harpy, Keg Runner and Barnacle Knight; Ember Isle's Cinder Skeleton, Magma Crab, Ash Bat and Obsidian Golem; Frostbite Reef's Frozen Corsair, Sled Penguin, Yeti Raider and Ice Witch; Fogbound Isle's Bone Deckhand, Fog Wraith, Cursed Cannoneer and Lantern Ghoul. (Skull Cove's Drowned Sailor is left out as the other three islands' basic walker already, its Thief Monkey because it steals gold rather than threatening the hull, and the Siren as a once-a-wave set piece.) A depth picks **2 types** at random, **one more every 5 depths** (3 from 6, 4 from 11 … **6** from 21). Each comes in its own group (`ABYSS.enemies`): Harpies in pairs, Ash Bats, Cinder Skeletons, Frozen Corsairs and Bone Deckhands in threes, Fog Wraiths in pairs, Sled Penguins in packs of 3–5, the rest alone, and a Lantern Ghoul with two Bone Deckhands in front for it to raise; Groups are picked by weight until they hold the depth's count, each counting as its type's **size** (how many enemies it's worth toward the count; default its members): Obsidian Golem 9 (it splits into two armoured crabs), Keg Runner pair 8, Ash Bat three 6, Storm Harpy pair 5, Barnacle Knight 5, Iron Crab 4, Magma Crab 4, Lantern Ghoul group 4, Yeti Raider, Ice Witch and Cursed Cannoneer 2; the walkers in threes and penguin packs count their heads. So a depth whose two types are Keg Runners and Harpies brings far fewer of them than a depth of Bone Deckhands brings deckhands. Weights: Keg Runners 0.5, Storm Harpies 0.5, Ash Bats 0.7, Ice Witches and Lantern Ghouls 0.5, the rest 1. Every enemy works as on its own island: penguins ram, witches shield, cannoneers shoot, golems split, ghouls raise. One exception: **hits that are a share of the hull** (Keg Runners blowing up, boarding boats) **take half their island share** here (`ABYSS.hullShare` 0.5: a Keg Runner 6%, an Elite one 11%, a boat 7.5%), so what ends a dive is what the crew can't kill, not a fixed share no upgrade or part outgrows. Lava bombs keep their 6%.
- **Elites** from depth 10: **10%** of enemies, rising evenly to **25% at depth 60** (and after); **x3 HP, x1.5 damage, x3 kill gold**, a slight violet tint (`0xc8a6ff`) and `elite_aura.png` (four 32x32 frames at 8 fps, looping) behind them, centred on the body. Bosses are never Elite.
- **Bosses** every 10 depths, taking turns: **the Kraken (10), Ghost Galleon (20), Molten Leviathan (30), Frost Wyrm (40), Frost Giant (50), Siren Queen (60), Captain Blackbones (70)**, then around again; **from depth 80 every boss depth brings a pair**: Kraken + Galleon (80), Leviathan + Wyrm (90), Giant + Siren Queen (100), Blackbones + a random other boss (110), and around. A boss depth has 65% of the usual count as escort (as on islands); the first boss enters 30% of the way through the groups, a second at 55%. Each boss has its own mechanics and its usual HP at the depth (no `bossBoost`), and its banner (at once, or as it arrives for those that announce themselves). Bosses drop **double** their material (6, or 10 the first time that boss is ever beaten), once per boss per depth, and no Pearls.
- **Checkpoints:** a dive starts at depth 1 or at any **multiple of 10 up to half the best depth**, rounded down (best 47: 1, 10 or 20). Starting at a checkpoint pays **that depth's gold** at once (its kills and clear bonus, as a clear would; shown on the "THE ABYSS / DEPTH 20 +1.23B GOLD" banner), so the crew isn't underpowered for it.
- **Abyss Tide:** each depth cleared in a dive adds a Tide stack, for that dive only (it resets when the dive ends); each stack is **+2% crew damage** (`ABYSS.tide`), on top of everything else, so a dive grows stronger as it goes. A small wood plaque right of the depth plaque counts them: "TIDE 7  +14%". Saved with the dive.
- **Skip the shallows:** a dive started at depth 1 with a best depth of **20** or more, and the card's **SKIP DEPTHS 1-5** toggle on (the default; saved), clears depths 1–5 at once: their gold (kills and clear bonus each) and 5 Tide stacks, no fight, and starts at depth 6 ("THE ABYSS / SKIPPED THE SHALLOWS - DEPTH 6 +… GOLD"). The toggle shows on the Abyss card with START AT 1 once it's allowed (`ABYSS.skip`); the card is 14px taller than an island's for it.
- **Auto-continue** works between depths like between waves; it pauses when a dive ends.
- **Currents** (stacking modifiers; icons in `currents.png`, 12x12, `abyss.json`'s order):

  | Current | Effect |
  |---|---|
  | Frenzied | Enemies move and attack 25% faster (`Enemy.pace`). |
  | Armoured | Enemies have +40% HP (and shields). |
  | Shielded | Every 4th enemy (not bosses) spawns with an Ice Witch's shield (40% of its max HP). |
  | Volcanic | Ember Isle's lava bombs (every 8–12s) the whole depth, with its ash. |
  | Blizzard | Frostbite Reef's blizzard rolls in 1.5s into the depth and blows to its end: a crewmate freezes every 8s for 2s (tap to break), with its snow; enemies don't slow down. |
  | Fogbound | Fogbound Isle's fog bank from 1.5s in to the depth's end: crew range -20% (the Blackbones' Lantern halves that), with its mist. |
  | Siren's Call | Every 15s a crewmate who attacks (not The Captain) is charmed for 2s (see Fogbound Isle's charms; tap to break). |
  | Risen Dead | A killed enemy (not a boss) has a 15% chance to rise as a Bone Deckhand where it fell. |

  Volcanic, Blizzard and Fogbound reuse the islands' own code (`Volcano`, `Blizzard`, `Mists`) on the depth's island-shaped stand-in. **Stacking:** one current from depth 5, two from 15, three from 30. At every multiple of 5 from 5, a new one joins if the count grows there (5, 15, 30); otherwise the **oldest is swapped** for a new random one, never the one it replaced (nor one already active). The dive's **seed** decides them (and each depth's types, Elites and line-up), so a dive can be replayed in the sim and a reloaded game meets the same depth. A current new at a depth is announced as it sets out: **"CURRENT: BLIZZARD"** with its effect under it (two at once: "CURRENTS" with both names; a dive's first depth announces all that are active), and a boss depth's banner follows 1.8s later.
- **Rewards:** each depth pays the normal gold for its effective wave (kills and clear bonus, with the Blackbones' Lantern); the banner says "DEPTH 12 CLEARED!" and the gold. **First-time milestones** (once per save, on clearing the depth): **10: a Rare Chest; 20: 2 Rare Chests; 30: a Legendary Chest; every 10 after: a Legendary Chest and 100 Pearls**, added to the banner and waiting in the chest screen as free chests.
- **The end:** when the ship sinks, "SHIP SUNK!" (or **"NEW RECORD - DEPTH 34"** if it went deeper than ever) with "THE ABYSS TOOK YOU AT DEPTH 34"; the ship is back at its island and 2s later the **end-of-run screen** opens: "THE DIVE IS OVER", **depth reached** (and "FROM 20" for a checkpoint start), **best depth** ("NEW RECORD!"), **gold earned** (checkpoint gold, kills and clears), **materials found** (icon and count each) and the **top crewmate** (the Captain's Log's damage summed over every depth of the dive: face, name and damage), with **Dive again** (a new dive, new seed, from the same start, or the deepest checkpoint still open below it) and **Map**. A dive's depth reached is the depth the ship sank on; the **best depth** is the deepest reached.
- **Captain's Log:** every depth is logged like a wave ("WAVE n" in its summary is the depth).
- **Saving:** the Abyss record (best depth, milestones paid, dives started, whether it was revealed) and the dive under way: its seed, the depth it's at, its start, and its totals so far (gold, materials, damage per crewmate) and the bosses whose material that depth has paid. Closing the game mid-depth resumes at the **start of that depth** ("THE ABYSS / BACK AT DEPTH n"); its kill gold is kept, like a lost wave's.
- **Dev:** "DEV OPEN ABYSS" marks every island cleared; in a dive "+10 WAVE" goes 10 depths deeper and "DEV NEXT BOSS WAVE" to the next boss depth.
- **Balance sim:** `npm run sim:abyss` (see Waves, Balance simulation, and `BALANCE_REPORT.md`).

## Expeditions

Crewmates can be sent on timed voyages that run in **real time** and come back with loot, even while the game is closed. Rules in `src/systems/Expeditions.js`, screens in `ExpeditionScene`, numbers in `EXPEDITIONS` (config), art and data in `public/sprites/ui/expedition/` (`expedition.json`: the destinations, each crewmate's trait and the trait icons' order).

- **Where:** a **Voyage** button (the 12px compass, `icon_expedition.png`, and the word "Voyage") in the bottom bar between Map and SET SAIL! (between waves), and a labelled one at the top right of the world map. It opens once **Skull Cove's wave 10 is cleared**; before that it's greyed out and tapping it says "Clear wave 10 to unlock" (a banner in battle, a line under the button on the map). A **red badge** counts the expeditions back and waiting to be collected; both buttons check every second.
- **Slots:** **1** at unlock, **2** once Ember Isle unlocks, **3** once Frostbite Reef unlocks (`slotIslands`). One expedition per slot.
- **Destinations:** 8, two per island (`expedition.json`), each with a 64x40 picture, a **favoured trait** and a **reward focus**. An island's destinations open on **reaching wave 25** there (its wave counter), except Skull Cove's, which open with the board at wave 10 (`destinationWaveByIsland`), so there's something to do from the start.

  | Destination | Island | Favours | Focus |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Coral Shallows | Skull Cove | Sailor | gold |
  | Smugglers' Cave | Skull Cove | Scout | chest |
  | Obsidian Mines | Ember Isle | Fighter | gold |
  | Hot Springs | Ember Isle | Caretaker | Pearls |
  | Frozen Wreck | Frostbite Reef | Scout | chest |
  | Aurora Peak | Frostbite Reef | Mystic | Pearls |
  | Ghost Market | Fogbound Isle | Mystic | mixed |
  | Blackbones' Vault | Fogbound Isle | Fighter | chest |

- **Durations:** **30 min (x1), 2 h (x3.2) or 8 h (x10)**: longer runs pay more per run but less per hour, so checking in often pays without punishing a night's sleep.
- **Traits:** every crewmate has one (`crewTraits`, by sprite name): **Fighter** (Cannoneer, The Duelist, The Captain, Anchor Bosun), **Sailor** (Cabin Boy, Net Thrower, Harpooner), **Scout** (Parrot Keeper, Sharpshooter, Powder Monkey, Whaler), **Mystic** (Voodoo Priestess, Ghost Pirate, Storm Caller, Fire Breather, Aurora Seer, Banshee, Bone Caller), **Caretaker** (Ship's Cook, Ship's Doctor, Grog Brewer, Blacksmith). The trait icon (`trait_icons.png`) shows on hero cards, in the hero picker after the name, on "Buy 10" tiles and on the expedition screens; the Crew Roster's info panel has a "TRAIT: SAILOR" tab.
- **The party:** 1–3 crewmates. Each one with the destination's favoured trait adds **+25%** to the rewards, and every star in the party **+5%** (party bonus = 1 + 0.25 x matches + 0.05 x stars, up to x2.5). A party of 3 all with the favoured trait always makes a **bonus find**.
- **Crew away:** crew on the ship can be sent; their slot empties until they come back, and the planning screen warns ("CABIN BOY WILL LEAVE THE DECK UNTIL THE EXPEDITION RETURNS"). Crew away can't be put on the ship: the hero picker lists them last, dimmed with the hourglass and "BACK IN 1:23:45", and won't pick them; roster cards show the hourglass and the time left (ticking). They stay away until the expedition is **collected**; then each goes back to the ship slot they left if it's still empty, else the first empty slot, else the bench. A save edited to put an away crewmate on the ship loads with that slot empty.
- **Rewards** (rolled when the party sets out, so reloading can't re-roll them; ±20% spread):
  - **Gold** = the gold per minute of battle over the **last 10 waves** (kills plus clear bonus over battle time in game time, won or lost; kept in the save) x **4** x the duration's multiplier x the focus's gold factor x the party bonus. So a 30-minute run is worth about 4 minutes of battle gold before the party bonus (a typical party of one match and a few stars lands near 6). Until 3 waves have been logged (a save from before expeditions) the gold per minute is estimated from the current wave's gold as if it took 60s.
  - **Pearls:** **3** on a 30-minute Pearls run, x the duration's multiplier and the party bonus.
  - Per focus: **gold** gold x1, no Pearls; **Pearls** Pearls x1 and gold x0.25; **chest** 1 chest and gold x0.25; **mixed** gold x0.5, Pearls x0.5 and a chest 15% of the time on a 30-minute run (48% on 2 h, certain on 8 h).
  - **Chests:** a plain Chest, except on **8 h runs**, where each chest found is a Chest 70%, a Rare Chest 25% or a Legendary Chest 5%. They wait in the chest screen as free chests (see Treasure Chests); the party bonus doesn't change chests.
  - **Bonus find:** guaranteed for a full matching party, otherwise **10%**: one extra reward revealed last. A Pearls run **doubles its Pearls**; any other finds a chest **one tier above** the best chest it found (a Rare Chest if it found none; a Legendary Chest stays Legendary).
  - Expeditions can't fail: a weak party just brings back less. How much gold expeditions bring, against playing, at the end of each island (gold per minute from the balance sim's greedy voyage, waves 41–50; per slot, gold focus, party bonus x1): a 30-min run every half hour brings 13% of an hour's battle gold at x1 speed, 2 h runs 11%, 8 h runs 8% (Skull Cove 92K / 74K / 58K an hour against 691K playing; Ember Isle 1.96M / 1.57M / 1.22M against 14.7M; Frostbite Reef 52M / 42M / 33M against 392M; Fogbound Isle 1.76B / 1.41B / 1.10B against 13.2B). The most possible, three slots of 30-min runs with x2.5 parties on both gold destinations and the Ghost Market, is 83% of playing at x1 (21% at x4), and it takes nine 5-star crew off the ship.
- **Time:** each expedition keeps how long it has run and when that was last advanced (`Date.now()`); every check adds the real time since. If the clock goes backwards it gains nothing (and loses nothing); a big jump forwards just finishes it. Saved, so it carries on while the game is closed.
- **Expedition board:** "EXPEDITIONS" over a card per slot (locked slots are wood: "SLOT LOCKED, OPENS WITH EMBER ISLE"). **Empty:** the compass and a gold **+ Send** (the whole card is tappable). **Out:** the destination's picture at 2x under its name, the party's faces with their trait icons, "8H EXPEDITION", the time left (H:MM:SS) and a bar. **Back:** a red **RETURNED!** stamp over the picture, "BACK WITH LOOT!" and a pulsing gold **Collect**. The line under the cards says "CREW KEEP SAILING WHILE THE GAME IS CLOSED", or how many are back. Countdowns tick in real time.
- **Planning screen:** "PLAN EXPEDITION" with Back. **Destination** (top left): the 8 pictures; locked ones show their island's black silhouette (its map picture) with "WAVE 25 / EMBER ISLE", and tapping one shows "REACH WAVE 25 ON EMBER ISLE" in the details. **Details** (top right): name, island, favoured trait (icon and name), reward focus, and the party bonus so far ("PARTY BONUS +55%", "2 SAILORS, 3 STARS", "FULL MATCH: BONUS FIND!"). **Crew** (middle): the party's 3 boxes (tap to take one off), then every crewmate as a tile (face, trait icon, ship slot number), favoured trait first (their trait icon glowing), strongest first, crew already away last (dimmed, hourglass, time left, not selectable); tapping a tile adds or removes them and shows their level, stars, trait and where they are. **Bottom:** the durations (**30M / 2H / 8H**), the reward preview ("~1.2K-1.8K gold", "3-5 PEARLS, 1 CHEST, MAYBE RARE+", "10% CHANCE OF A BONUS FIND" / "BONUS FIND GUARANTEED!") and a gold **Send**. Every tap target is at least 24px. It remembers the last destination and duration.
- **Reveal** (Collect): the loot is paid and saved first, so closing mid-reveal loses nothing. The destination's picture at 2x with the party standing in front of it (fading in one by one), a one-line **story** on a parchment strip, picked from 4 per destination with a crewmate's name ("Harpooner hauled in something enormous.", `EXPEDITIONS.stories`), then the rewards as face-down wood cards ("?") that rattle and **flip over one at a time** (0.65s apart): gold, Pearls, each chest (glowing in its tier's colour), and the bonus find last, edged in gold, with a gold **"BONUS FIND!"** banner, the Legendary chime, a flash and confetti. Each flip plays `sfx.loot` (a coin jingle, a bubbly shimmer, a chest's reveal chime, the Legendary chime). Tapping anywhere turns everything over at once. Then **Done** and **Send again** (the same party, destination and duration in one tap), and "CHEST WAITING IN THE CHEST SCREEN" if there were chests.
- **Dev:** "DEV END EXPEDITIONS" with the other dev buttons brings every expedition back at once.
- **The balance sim ignores expeditions** (it never sends any, and its runs match the ones before expeditions wave for wave).
- **Boss materials:** a run to one of an island's destinations has a **15%** chance of bringing back **1–2** of one of that island's boss materials (picked at random among its bosses beaten at least once; none before any is), as an extra reward card after the chests: the material's icon at 2x, "+2" and its name (`PARTS.expedition`). Rolled with the rest of the loot when the party sets out, and kept in the save with it.

## Ship Parts

Every boss drops its own **material**, materials make **ship parts**, and parts show on the ship and change how fights play. Data in `src/systems/Parts.js` (what exists, costs), owned state in `Progress`, battle effects in `src/systems/ShipParts.js`, the screen in `ShipyardScene`; numbers in `PARTS` (config); art and data in `public/sprites/parts/` (`parts.json`: the materials in `materials.png`'s order, the slots, and each part's slot, material, effect text, overlay and icon).

- **Materials** (16x16 icons in `materials.png`), one per boss: **Kraken Ink** (The Kraken), **Spectral Ectoplasm** (The Ghost Galleon), **Magma Core** (Molten Leviathan), **Wyrm Scale** (Frost Wyrm), **Frozen Heartwood** (Frost Giant), **Siren Pearl** (Siren Queen), **Cursed Doubloon** (Captain Blackbones). A material (and the parts made from it) only shows once its boss is in the game: an `ENEMIES` boss with that sprite on an island with content (all seven are now).
- **Drops:** every boss killed in a wave drops **3** of its material, **5** the first time that boss is ever beaten (its Wanted Board defeat count was 0), floating up green as "+3 KRAKEN INK" over the gold. The finales' bosses each drop their own the same way. Like boss Pearls, each boss pays once per wave: killing it and then losing the wave doesn't pay it again on the retry (each island record keeps the last wave each boss paid, `dropsPaid`). Boss Hunts pay their own (see Boss Hunts); expeditions find a few (see Expeditions).
- **Where they show:** a **Materials** strip down the left edge of the chest screen (icon and count per material, never-found ones dark with a dash; hidden while a Buy 10 grid covers it), along the bottom of the Shipyard (hover an icon for its name), and on a boss's Wanted Board page ("DROPS: [icon] KRAKEN INK" under its description).
- **Slots and parts:** four slots, **Figurehead, Sails, Cannons, Lantern**, one part on each (or none: the stock look). Seven parts:

## Boss Hunts

The reason to sail back to an island: a single fight against one of its bosses, with its normal enemies trickling in, using the crew and ship as they are now. `HUNTS` in config; `Progress` keeps the levels, `composeWave` builds the fight (`huntPlan`).

- **Where:** on the world map, an island's card gets two tabs over its top edge, **ISLAND** and **BOSS HUNT**, once any of its bosses has been beaten at least once (Skull Cove: The Kraken and the Ghost Galleon; Ember Isle: the Molten Leviathan; Frostbite Reef: the Frost Wyrm and the Frost Giant; Fogbound Isle: the Siren Queen and Captain Blackbones). The Boss Hunt tab lists each with its 32x32 portrait: beaten ones with "2X TODAY: 3 LEFT" (green; "NO 2X LEFT TODAY" once used), "LV 4  WIN [icon] +3" and a gold **HUNT** button; the rest dark, "???", "BEAT IT IN A BOSS WAVE TO HUNT IT HERE". A line at the bottom says each win adds 8% HP and damage.
- **The fight:** HUNT closes the map, sails there if the ship is elsewhere ("SAILING TO …") and starts at once (the Flyers ahead! check still applies). It's the boss's **first wave on that island** (wave 10 for the first boss, 20 for the second) at that wave's plain scaling with its island offset (**no `bossBoost` or `earlyBoost`**): the boss comes in 1.5s in, and that island's formations trickle in, 60% of that wave's usual count, a group every 3s from 4s, Elites as on that wave. The island's own hazards come too where they aren't tied to wave numbers (Ember Isle's lava bombs); blizzards and fog banks skip boss waves, so there are none. The banner says "BOSS HUNT - LV 3" over the boss's name, and the wave plaque "HUNT LV 3".
- **Hunt level:** each boss has its own, from **1**. Everyone's HP and damage in the fight are x **1.08^(level - 1)**. A win raises it by 1; a loss costs nothing and doesn't lower it. No energy and no limits: the rising difficulty is the limit.
- **Win:** **2 + floor(level / 3)** of the boss's material (the level fought), **doubled for the first 3 wins over each boss each day** (local time, reset at midnight; `huntDay` and `huntWins` in the save), plus a little gold: 2 wave-clear bonuses of the fight's wave (with the Blackbones' Lantern's bonus), on top of kill gold. "THE KRAKEN HUNTED!" with "+4 KRAKEN INK (2X TODAY)  +179 GOLD  -  NEXT: LV 2". A hunt pays no boss Pearls and no wave material drop, doesn't advance the wave, and stays out of the Captain's Log and the gold per minute expeditions use (the MVP plaque still shows). **Loss:** "HUNT FAILED", "THE KRAKEN GOT AWAY - TRY AGAIN ANY TIME".
- **Auto-continue** stays paused through a hunt and isn't resumed by it (opening the map paused it), so nothing starts by itself after one.

## No prestige

There is no prestige or reset loop: progress is one long voyage across the islands. (A "New Voyage" prestige with a Renown currency and a Renown shop existed up to save v5 and was removed. Loading an older save drops it and pays back all Renown, held plus everything spent on shop levels, at **2 Pearls per Renown**; a voyage at wave 20 paid 10 Renown, so that is worth about 20 Pearls, roughly 6 chests.)

## Crew Roster (collection book)

- Opened from the **Crew** button between waves.
- Shows every hero in the game (22, the pack crew included) as small cards, five to a row and ten to a page, grouped by rarity, with an "N/M FOUND" count beside the title plate. With more than ten heroes, arrows either side of the title plate turn the page.
- A name too wide for its card shows the short name ("Voodoo").
- Owned heroes appear as their card with level and stars (and "+N" Bonus Levels), their trait icon, and the hourglass and time left while away on an expedition. Clicking one shows it as a large card beside an Ability panel (name, cooldown, effect at its current level and stars) with a "TRAIT: SAILOR" tab under it; click anywhere to close. Unowned heroes are black silhouettes with "???" for the name, so the player can see how many are left to find without spoiling who they are.

## Wanted Board (enemy book)

- Opened from the **Wanted** button between waves (at the right end of the bottom bar; its icon is `icon_wanted.png`). A red badge on the button counts the posters that haven't been opened yet.
- Art in `public/sprites/ui/wanted/`; poster positions (enemy box, stamp spot, selection frame offset) come from its `wanted.json`.
- **Posters:** one per enemy type (not boarding boats or lava bombs), four to a row and twelve to a page in the order they're first met on the voyage (Skull Cove's by wave, then Ember Isle's, Frostbite Reef's and Fogbound Isle's), with an "N/M FOUND" count beside the title plate; with more than twelve, arrows either side of the title plate turn the page. A met type's poster is `poster.png` with frame 0 of its sheet in the enemy box (the bottom 30 rows of a 32x32 portrait, so feet show); Frostbite Reef's and Fogbound Isle's types use their single `frost/<name>.png` / `fog/<name>.png` instead of the sheet. The Kraken, the Siren, the Ghost Galleon (its skull bow), the Molten Leviathan, the Frost Wyrm, the Frost Giant, the Siren Queen and Captain Blackbones (their heads) use a 32x32 crop of that picture (`face` in config). The highest bounty earned shows as a bronze / silver / gold stamp, and a red dot marks a poster not opened yet. Types not met yet are `poster_unknown.png` and can't be opened.
- **Details page** (parchment, right): clicking a poster frames it (`poster_select.png`), marks it seen and shows its page: name on a wood plate, its portrait at 2x, trait chips (icon + label: Boss, Flying, Armoured, Shielded, Explodes, Support (the Siren, the Ice Witch, the Lantern Ghoul), by what the enemy does), HP / speed / damage at the current wave (from its multipliers on the current island; "-" for none; "12% HULL" and the like for Keg Runners) and its first wave ("FIRST WAVE" on Skull Cove, "EMBER ISLE WAVE" for Ember Isle's types and "FROSTBITE WAVE" for Frostbite Reef's, "FOGBOUND WAVE" for Fogbound Isle's, from the island's `shortName` when it has one), a one-line description (`description` in config), "Weak to" with the faces and short names of the crew good against it (`weakTo` in config), and the bounty: the three stamps (dim until paid), defeats so far, and a bar to the next tier. A boss's page also says what it drops for ship parts under its description ("DROPS: [icon] KRAKEN INK"). A legend under the posters lists the bounty tiers.
- **Meeting a new type:** the first time an enemy type spawns, its poster unlocks and a "NEW ENEMY!" alert shows at the top centre for 3s (its poster, name and description on a wood plaque, fading in and out), without pausing the game. Several in a row queue up. It runs on real time, so it lasts as long at x2 speed.
- **Defeats and bounties:** every kill of a type counts (Elites too; Keg Runners blowing up on the hull and Thief Monkeys getting away don't). Reaching 25 / 250 / 1,000 defeats pays 10 / 25 / 50 Pearls at once, with a small toast under the wave plaque (the stamp, the type and count, and the Pearls) that fades after 2.5s; toasts stack.

## Captain's Log (battle stats)

- Opened from the **Log** button (wood, book icon) at the left end of the bottom bar between waves. A parchment panel under a "CAPTAIN'S LOG" plate, with three tab buttons (the open one gold, the others wood) and a line under them with a summary on the left and the bar legend (or a note) on the right. Six rows show at a time; with more, the mouse wheel and two arrows at the right of that line scroll them.
- **Last Wave / Last 5 Waves:** one row per crewmate, highest damage first: their face and name, a damage bar (scaled so the top row fills it) in three segments: **attacks** (orange), **abilities** (blue) and **poison & effects** (green), then their total and share of the crew's damage, and a small line with kills and ability uses, plus their support stat if they have one: The Captain "AURA ADDED N DMG", the Ship's Doctor "REPAIRED N HULL", the Net Thrower "SLOWED N ENEMIES", the Ship's Cook "N STUNS APPLIED". The top row gets a gold **MVP** badge. The summary reads "WAVE 12 CLEARED" / "WAVE 12 SUNK", or "5 WAVES: 4 WON, 1 LOST" (Last 5 Waves sums each crewmate over the waves kept).
- **Damage Taken:** one row per enemy type that hurt the hull over the waves kept (its poster picture), most first, with a red bar, the hull HP it took and its share; the summary gives the total hull lost.
- **What counts:** every wave, won or lost (not Boss Hunts). Only damage actually dealt counts: the part of a hit that a dying enemy didn't need (overkill) is left out, and damage to a Barnacle Knight's shield counts up to what the shield had left. Splash, chain lightning and the Harpooner's pierce count as the hitter's attacks; Broadside, Whale Harpoon, Tidal Wave, Flock, Deadeye and Lunge's crit shots as their ability; poison ticks (the Grog Brewer's bottles and Grog Barrel's puddle) and a curse's bonus damage (her attacks and Hex) as the poisoner's or curser's effects, so the Voodoo Priestess is credited with the extra damage her curses add to everyone's hits. A Keg Runner's blast goes to whoever killed the keg. A kill goes to whoever dealt the killing blow (poison included).
- **Support stats:** The Captain's aura bonus is the part of each buffed crewmate's damage his deck buff added (from its damage bonus, and for attacks its attack speed bonus too: a hit worth d with a x1.88 damage, x1.3 speed buff counts d x (1 - 1 / (1.88 x 1.3)) for him); his All Hands! isn't counted. The Doctor's is hull HP Patch Up actually repaired; the Net Thrower's counts each enemy caught by a net or Big Net; the Cook's each stun applied by his pans or Hot Stew.
- **Hull damage** is the HP each hit actually took off (so a killing hit counts only what was left), by enemy type; boarding boats and lava bombs count as their own types, and a charmed crewmate's hits on the hull count as the Siren Queen's. Shooting down a lava bomb isn't counted as a kill.
- **MVP plaque:** after every wave (won or lost), a wood plaque with the top damage dealer's face and "MVP: <name> - <damage> dmg" shows under the wave banner for 2.5s, fading in and out. It takes no clicks and runs on real time; no plaque if nobody dealt damage.
- **Cost:** tracking is a few additions per hit into a per-crewmate entry through shared source objects, so big waves don't slow down. The last 5 waves are kept in the save (`BATTLE_LOG` in config).

## Between-wave UI

Layout follows `ui_mock_battle.png` and `ui_mock_between_waves.png`.

- **Numbers:** every number the player sees (gold, Pearls, hull HP, costs, damage, buffs, levels, rewards, Log stats, floating gold, bounties, chest prices) goes through one formatter, `formatNum` in `src/ui/format.js`. Below 10,000 it's the whole number with commas (9,876; never decimals, so 1065.6 damage reads 1,066); from 10,000 it's three significant digits and a suffix, K, M, B, T, then aa, ab ... az, ba ... (12.3K, 353M, 1.07B, 4.56ab), cut rather than rounded so 999,999 reads 999K. So nothing is ever wider than six characters.
- **Anchoring:** the HUD is laid out in the 480x270 base and each group sticks to its part of the game area (never the bars): the wave and hull plaques to the top-left corner, the gold and Pearls plaques and their buttons to the top-right, the enemies-left bar to the top centre, the Shipwright (and hero picker) to the right, and the bottom button bar (and speed button) to the bottom-right, under the Shipwright, the auto-continue toggle to the bottom-left, above the Log button, and the dev buttons (development builds) above it. Overlays (Chests, Crew Roster, confirmations) stay centred with their dark backdrop covering the whole view.
- **Always:** top-left, a wood plaque with the island's short name and the wave after the skull ("FOGBOUND · WAVE 15", the dot drawn in pixels; red "BOSS" on boss waves), as wide as it needs between 110 and 166px; if that doesn't fit, the island's map art (`map/island_<id>.png`, scaled to 16px wide) takes the skull's place and the name goes ("WAVE 150 BOSS"). Under it the hull HP bar sits on its own small wood plaque (so it never sits on the moon or sky), "40.5K / 40.5K", the bar as wide as the widest formatted HP needs ("88.8aa / 88.8aa"). Top-right, two wood plaques side by side, each with its icon and room for a six-character number: gold on the left (82px, the wider) and Pearls (74px), with a settings button (gear: Reset progress, with a confirmation, between waves only), a sound toggle and a fullscreen toggle under it. Short messages (WAVE N CLEARED!, SHIP SUNK!, THE KRAKEN RISES!) appear in the big font on a wood plaque over the sky, some with a small detail line (gold and Pearls earned). Crew have no name labels on the ship.
- **During a wave:** an enemies-left bar at top-centre ("N ENEMIES LEFT", the fill shrinking as the wave is beaten); the **ability bar** at the bottom centre: a 26px wood button per crewmate on the ship, in slot order, with their face (cropped from their sprite), their key number (1–6) in the corner, a dark clockwise cooldown sweep over the face with the seconds left, and a pulsing 2px gold glow when ready, then the **AUTO** toggle (gold text and glow when on); and a speed button bottom-right showing the current speed (x1 / x2 / x4); clicking it cycles through them (`GAME_SPEEDS` in config). At x2 everything in the battle runs twice as fast (x4 four times): movement, attacks, spawns, animations and effects. The speed is remembered for the rest of the session (not saved), and between waves the game always runs at normal speed. The between-wave panel and buttons are hidden.
- **Timing:** the battle runs in fixed steps of 1/240 s of game time (`SIM` in config): each frame's real time (counted as at most 0.1s, so a backgrounded tab doesn't teleport enemies) times the battle speed is spent in as many whole steps as fit, the rest carried to the next frame. Movement, attacks, cooldowns, spawns and ability effects all advance per step, so a wave plays out exactly the same at 30, 60 or 144 fps and at any battle speed. (Purely visual things, such as animations, tweens, NEW ENEMY! alerts and the storm, follow the frame clock.)
- **Auto-continue** (bottom-left corner, between waves and during them): a small wood toggle, "AUTO-CONTINUE OFF / ON / PAUSED". With it on, 2s after a wave is cleared the next one starts by itself, and 2s after a loss the wave is retried (Flyers ahead! is skipped). Pressing any other button (except the speed and ability buttons) or a ship slot pauses it, shown as PAUSED, until a wave is started by hand with SET SAIL!. A Boss Hunt doesn't resume it, and it doesn't start anything after one. Turning it on between waves starts the 2s countdown straight away. It lasts for the session (not saved). `AUTO_CONTINUE` in config; `src/ui/AutoContinue.js`.
- **Dev tools (development builds, or the live site with `?dev` in the URL, e.g. `https://tkg111.github.io/hold-the-deck/?dev`):** in the bottom-left corner of the view: "DEV ALL HEROES", "+10 PRL" and "+10 WAVE" between waves, plus "DEV SAIL: <island>" (sails on to the next island with content, back to Skull Cove after the last, marking every island before it cleared so it's unlocked; this is saved) "DEV NEXT BOSS WAVE" (skips ahead to the next boss wave or the island's finale, never past the finale) and "DEV END EXPEDITIONS" (every expedition comes back now), "DEV +50 MATERIALS" (50 more of every boss material) and "DEV OPEN ABYSS" (every island cleared, so the Abyss opens; in a dive "+10 WAVE" goes 10 depths deeper and "DEV NEXT BOSS WAVE" to the next boss depth), stacked above the auto-continue toggle. - **Shipwright** (between waves only, `src/ui/Shipwright.js`): a 236px parchment panel under a wood title plate. Along its top, two tabs, **Ship** (a **Parts** row that opens the Shipyard, then Hull and Build deck; see Ship Parts) and **Crew** (the crew on the ship in slot order, then every other owned crewmate), and a three-way buy toggle, **x1 / x10 / MAX** (the open tab and the chosen amount are gold buttons). Each row: icon or face, name with its level after it ("LV 86"; Build deck shows "2/3"), and a line with what the buy changes, from now to after it: "HP 40.5K → 43.3K", "DMG 1.07K → 1.12K", "BUFF +100% → +102%" (The Captain), "SLOTS 2 → 4". The gold buy button on the right shows the price, or with x10 / MAX the levels and their total ("x7 21.4M"). x10 buys 10 levels (fewer if the upgrade caps first, e.g. the last deck) only if all of them are affordable; MAX buys as many as the gold covers. Out of reach, the button is the disabled gold one with the price in red; affordable rows get a small green ▲ after the level. A label too wide for its button drops the coin icon, then steps down to the small font, so it can never run past the edge. A name too long for the row uses the crewmate's short name (only the Voodoo Priestess, "Voodoo"). Maxed rows ("Build deck 3/3", "ALL DECKS BUILT", MAX) sink to the bottom of their tab, dimmed.
